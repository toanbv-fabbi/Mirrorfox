## ⚡ What is Mirrorfox?

**Mirrorfox** is a high-performance desktop application engineered for **iOS Developers, QA Engineers, UI/UX Designers, and Remote Teams** to mirror iPhone screens to Windows, macOS, and Linux with wire-speed 60 FPS performance and <15ms latency.

Beyond ultra-smooth mirroring, Mirrorfox transforms your desktop into an **all-in-one iPhone QA Workbench**: broadcast your iPhone screen as a native system **Virtual Camera** into Zoom/Teams/OBS, sideload `.ipa` test builds in 3 seconds without Xcode, inspect pixel-perfect colors, verify Apple HIG typography and Safe Area guidelines, and diff live screens against Figma designs.

> 🔒 **Zero iPhone Installation Required:** Connect via standard Lightning / USB-C cable or Wi-Fi AirPlay. No companion app, no test profiles, no enterprise certificates, and no jailbreak needed.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 📱 1. Wire-Speed 60 FPS Mirroring (USB & Wireless AirPlay)
* **Hardware-Accelerated USB Tunnel**: Utilizes native hardware usbmuxd tunnel for rock-solid 60 FPS 4K screen mirroring with under 15ms latency.
* **AirPlay 2 Wireless Mode**: Instant zero-cable mirroring over local Wi-Fi.
* **Auto-Orientation**: Smoothly rotates between Portrait and Landscape stances for testing apps and gaming.

### 📹 2. System-Level Virtual Camera (Windows & macOS)
* **DirectShow & Media Foundation**: Turns your live iPhone screen into a native system webcam on Windows 10/11 and macOS.
* **Zero Plugin Required**: Select `Mirrorfox Virtual Camera` directly inside **Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, and OBS Studio**.
* **Crystal-Clear Demos**: Perfect for client presentations, remote sprint reviews, QA bug demonstrations, and mobile live-streaming.

### 📦 3. Drag & Drop .IPA Sideloading in 3 Seconds
* **No Xcode or Mac Needed**: Simply drag and drop any development or Ad-Hoc `.ipa` file onto the window.
* **Smart Provisioning Inspector**: Automatically verifies Bundle ID, minimum iOS version, architectures, and checks whether the connected device's UDID is included in the embedded mobileprovision profile before installing.

### 🎨 4. Pixel Loupe & Color Accuracy
* **8× Pixel Magnifier**: Hover anywhere to zoom in on individual display pixels.
* **Multi-Color Format Inspection**: Inspect exact color values in **HEX, RGB, and Display P3**.
* **Instant Clipboard Copy**: Press `Ctrl+C` (Windows) or `⌘C` (macOS) to copy color codes directly into your CSS, Tailwind, or Flutter theme.

### 🔍 5. Figma Pixel Diffing & Design Verification
* **Live Design Overlay**: Drag an exported Figma frame or PNG onto the mirroring stage.
* **Adjustable Split Slider & Opacity**: Slide between the live running app and the design spec to catch 1px padding misalignments, wrong icons, and layout shifts before design review.

### 🔤 6. Apple HIG Typography & Safe Area Rulers
* **Typography Inspector**: Verify font sizes, font weights, line-heights, and kerning against Apple Human Interface Guidelines.
* **Interactive 2D Rulers & Safe Area Overlay**: Toggle pixel guides for Dynamic Island, Notch, status bar, and home indicator.

### 🎬 7. Multi-Device Stage (1–4 iPhones) & Step-by-Step Bug Recording
* **Multi-Window / Pop-Out Windows**: Connect up to 4 iPhones simultaneously (via USB and Wi-Fi) and pop them out into floating windows across multiple monitors.
* **Live-Baked Annotations**: Draw freehand lines, arrows, rectangles, and laser pointers directly while presenting.
* **Embedded Annotations**: All drawings, notes, and step markers are baked directly into recordings and screenshots for instant Jira, Linear, or GitHub issue reporting.

## 💻 System Requirements

* **Linux**: Ubuntu 22.04+, Debian 12+, Fedora 38+
* **iOS Devices**: iPhone and iPad running iOS 14.0 through iOS 18+
* **Cables**: Standard Apple Lightning or USB-C to USB-A/C cable

## 🔒 Privacy & Security

* **100% Local Processing**: All video streaming, sideloading, and analysis occur strictly on your local machine.
